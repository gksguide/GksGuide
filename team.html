About

The GKS scholarship is one of the most competitive government scholarships in the world, and the application path is full of details, ambiguities, and unanswered questions.

I am Sania Sencholi (Saniasci), a specialist consultant and mentor for the South Korean Government Scholarship (GKS), and I have worked exclusively in this field since 2021.
I began working exclusively on academic opportunities in South Korea and the GKS scholarship in 2021, and this path took shape with the launch of the kgsp-iran Telegram channel. Today, the channel has become one of the leading specialist references on the South Korean government scholarship among Iranian and Afghan (Persian-speaking) applicants. It brings together the latest GKS news and updates, analyses of the official guidelines, practical files and guides, real experiences of accepted and rejected applicants, answers to frequently asked questions, and important tips you will rarely find elsewhere, all in one place.
Since 2022, I have worked as an honorary reporter for the South Korean Ministry of Culture, and in 2023 I was selected as one of the top 10 Iranian reporters and officially honored by the Embassy of South Korea. This direct connection with Korean sources helps me give you information that is more accurate and up to date than what is available publicly.
In 2025, I also won a free three-day trip with the Netflix Korea program to promote the series Squid Game. In 2026, I took part in an online exchange course at Sungkyunkwan University in Seoul, one of the top ten universities in Korea. The course covered media in South Korea and the Hallyu (Korean Wave), and its outcome was a certificate of participation and an official transcript from Sungkyunkwan University itself.
Over the years, I have provided specialized GKS consulting in many areas: from reviewing résumés and completing application forms, to preparing documents, giving feedback on and editing personal statements and study plans, guiding applicants in writing GKS recommendation letters, and answering questions about the application path. Every year, applicants who used these services have been accepted at every level: associate degree, bachelor's, master's, and doctoral. So far, more than 20 Iranian applicants, 2 applicants from Afghanistan, and several from Tajikistan and Russia have received the GKS scholarship through this team's consulting. For details, see the Success Stories section.
